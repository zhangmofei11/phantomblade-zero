Boss Fight Tips

Study First

Watch enemy attacks for a full cycle before engaging. Learn the audio and visual cues.

Sha-Chi Management

Save Sha-Chi for punish windows. Don't waste it on low-damage trades.

Heal Wisely

Heal only in safe windows. Attacking during enemy recovery is always better.
