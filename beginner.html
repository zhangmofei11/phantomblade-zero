Beginner Guide

Everything new players need to know before starting Phantom Blade Zero.

Essential Tips

1. Parry, Don't Dodge

New players spam dodge and get punished. Focus on learning parry timing first. Blue flash = parry.

2. Ghoststep for Red Flash

When you see red flash, dodge won't save you. Use Ghoststep (R1/E) to dash through unblockable attacks.

3. Don't Hoard Sha-Chi

Sha-Chi decays over time. Build it up, then immediately spend on heavy attacks or special abilities.

4. Start on Normal

Hard mode punishes mistakes severely. Learn the mechanics on Normal first, then switch to Hard.

Difficulty Modes

Story Mode

For players focused on narrative. Minimal challenge.

  • • 50% damage taken
  • • Infinite healing items
  • • Simplified combat

Normal Mode

Recommended for most players. Balanced challenge.

  • • Standard damage
  • • Limited healing
  • • Full combat depth

Hard Mode

For veterans seeking challenge. Significantly harder.

  • • 150% damage taken
  • • Limited healing
  • • Bosses have new patterns

Character Stats

STR

Increases heavy attack damage and posture break

SKI

Increases attack speed and critical chance

ARC

Increases Sha-Chi generation and capacity

END

Increases stamina pool and resistance

Recommended Starting Build

For beginners: Equal STR and SKI with some END. This gives you good damage while maintaining stamina for parries.

Early Game Advice

First 30 Minutes

  • • Complete the tutorial - it teaches core mechanics well
  • • Choose Longsword as your first weapon - most forgiving
  • • Don't skip the first boss - it teaches parry timing
  • • Collect all Memory Fragments - they unlock lore and bonuses

Resource Management

  • • Healing items are limited - use them strategically
  • • Save often at shrines - they also restore health
  • • Collect Jade Tablets - needed for weapon upgrades
  • • Don't sell materials - you'll need them later

Combat Practice

  • • Practice parry timing on regular enemies first
  • • Learn to read enemy attack wind-ups
  • • Focus on one enemy's attacks at a time in groups
  • • Heavy attacks interrupt most enemy attacks

Common Beginner Mistakes

  • • Button mashing - Phantom Blade rewards deliberate play
  • • Ignoring the blue flash - parry windows are generous
  • • Rolling into attacks - Ghoststep is safer
  • • Fighting exhausted - manage your stamina

Quick Controls Reference

Light Attack R2 / Left Click
Heavy Attack R2 + R1 / Right Click
Parry L1 / Q
Ghoststep R1 / E
Heal Touch Pad / H
Lock On R3 / Tab
