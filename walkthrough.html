Main Story Chapters

Ch1

The Awakening

Begin your journey as the Phantom. Learn basic combat and meet key allies.

Objectives

  • • Escape the prison compound
  • • Find the hidden weapon cache
  • • Defeat The Scarred Monk (Boss)
  • • Meet your contact in town

Collectibles

  • • 3 Memory Fragments
  • • 5 Jade Tablets
  • • 1 Weapon Schematic
Ch2

The Blade Dancer

Track down your rival Yi Ling. Master Ghoststep mechanics.

Objectives

  • • Investigate the trading post
  • • Track Yi Ling through the forest
  • • Survive the ambush
  • • Defeat Blade Dancer Yi Ling (Boss)

Collectibles

  • • 4 Memory Fragments
  • • 3 Jade Tablets
  • • Ghoststep Upgrade
Ch3

The Iron Fortress

Infiltrate the Black Iron Fortress. Heavy combat encounters.

Objectives

  • • Scout the fortress perimeter
  • • Infiltrate via the sewers
  • • Disable the alarm bells
  • • Defeat Commander Zhou (Boss)

Collectibles

  • • 5 Memory Fragments
  • • 4 Jade Tablets
  • • Heavy Armor Set
Ch4

The Poison Assassin

Hunt the shadow assassin. Stealth and environmental kills.

Objectives

  • • Track the assassin through the village
  • • Survive the poison traps
  • • Corner her in the bathhouse
  • • Defeat The Poison Assassin (Boss)

Collectibles

  • • 4 Memory Fragments
  • • 6 Jade Tablets
  • • Poison Resistance Charm
Ch5

The Corrupted Dragon

Journey to the mountain shrine. Face a mythical beast.

Objectives

  • • Climb the mountain path
  • • Find the shrine entrance
  • • Solve the elemental puzzle
  • • Defeat The Corrupted Dragon (Boss)

Collectibles

  • • 6 Memory Fragments
  • • 5 Jade Tablets
  • • Dragon Scale Armor
Ch6

The Black Iron Lord

Assault the fortress throne. Two-phase boss battle.

Objectives

  • • Rally the resistance forces
  • • Fight through the throne room
  • • Reach the Lord
  • • Defeat Lord of Black Iron (Boss)

Collectibles

  • • 5 Memory Fragments
  • • 4 Jade Tablets
  • • Legendary Weapon Piece
Ch7

The Phantom

Confront your past. Final chapter with multiple ending branches.

Objectives

  • • Make critical story choices
  • • Face the trials
  • • Defeat your past self
  • • Choose your ending

Collectibles

  • • 8 Memory Fragments
  • • All Jade Tablets
  • • Ending-Dependent Items

Ending Paths

Your choices in Chapter 7 determine which of the 8 endings you receive. Key decision points:

  • • Choice 1: Save or spare the captured ally
  • • Choice 2: Trust or confront the informant
  • • Choice 3: Embrace or reject the Phantom legacy
