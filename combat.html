Parry System

The core defensive mechanic in Phantom Blade Zero. When you see a blue flash before an enemy's attack lands, that's your window to parry.

How to Parry

  • • Watch for the blue flash indicator
  • • Press L1 (PS5) or Q (PC) just before impact
  • • Successful parry staggers the enemy
  • • Perfect timing creates a larger window

Benefits

  • • Deals posture damage to enemies
  • • Creates opening for counterattacks
  • • Builds Sha-Chi meter
  • • Most efficient way to fight

Ghoststep System

When you see a red flash, use Ghoststep instead of parrying. This dash through attacks cannot be parried.

How to Ghoststep

  • • Press R1 (PS5) or E (PC) + direction
  • • Dashes through the attack
  • • Brief invincibility during dash
  • • Ends behind the enemy

Tactical Uses

  • • Avoid unblockable attacks
  • • Reposition behind enemies
  • • Escape crowd situations
  • • Chain into counterattacks

Sha-Chi System

Sha-Chi is Phantom Blade Zero's resource system. Build it with light attacks, spend it on devastating heavy attacks.

Building Sha-Chi

  • • Light attack combos
  • • Successful parries
  • • Perfect dodges
  • • Breaking enemy posture

Spending Sha-Chi

  • • Heavy attacks cost 1-2 bars
  • • Special abilities cost 2-3 bars
  • • Ultimate attack costs full bar
  • • Sha-Chi decays over time

Maximize Damage

  • • Build then immediately spend
  • • Combo: Parry → Heavy → Special
  • • Ultimate at full bar for max DPS
  • • Don't let it decay!

Basic Combos

Starter Combo

Light → Light → Light → Heavy

Safe opening combo. Ends with knockdown.

Parry Counter

Parry → Heavy → Light → Ghoststep

High damage from parry opening.

Sha-Chi Burst

Build 3 bars → Heavy × 3 → Ultimate

Maximum damage combo.

Ghoststep Mixup

Ghoststep → Backstab → Heavy

Positional advantage combo.
