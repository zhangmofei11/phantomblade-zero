Key Decision Points

Chapter 7 - The Phantom

The final chapter presents three major choices. Your combinations determine your ending.

Choice 1

The Captured Ally

Save - Attempt rescue
Spare - Leave them behind

Choice 2

The Informant

Trust - Believe their story
Confront - Demand the truth

Choice 3

The Phantom Legacy

Embrace - Accept your destiny
Reject - Forge your own path

All Endings

👑

True Ending - The Redeemed Phantom

Best

Save ally + Trust informant + Reject legacy

Abe destroys the Phantom organization from within, frees all captives, and chooses to live as a normal person. The cycle of violence ends.

🌅

Good Ending - New Dawn

Good

Save ally + (Trust or Confront) + Embrace legacy

Abe destroys the Phantom organization but accepts a leadership role to guide it toward justice. Bitter-sweet but hopeful.

🌙

Neutral Ending - The Wanderer

Neutral

Spare ally + Trust informant + Reject legacy

Abe walks away from the Phantom life, leaving the organization intact but weakened. A solitary ending.

⚔️

Neutral Ending - The Vengeful

Neutral

Save ally + Confront informant + Reject legacy

Abe achieves revenge but loses the ally in the process. Hollow victory with no future direction.

🗡️

Bad Ending - The Tyrant

Bad

Spare ally + Trust informant + Embrace legacy

Abe becomes the new Phantom Lord but is corrupted by power. The cycle of violence continues under a new tyrant.

💀

Bad Ending - Death by Deception

Bad

Spare ally + Trust informant + Reject legacy

The informant betrays Abe. The ally dies. Abe is killed before realizing the truth.

🔥

Bad Ending - Consumed by Vengeance

Bad

Save ally + Confront informant + Embrace legacy

Abe destroys everything in pursuit of revenge, including the ally. Becomes the very thing they fought against.

☠️

Worst Ending - Mutual Destruction

Worst

Spare ally + Confront informant + Embrace legacy

Abe's betrayal is met with betrayal. Everyone dies. The Phantom organization crumbles but so does everything Abe loved.

Tips for Completionists

  • • Save before key choices - You can reload to try different paths
  • • Collect all Memory Fragments - Some endings require full understanding
  • • Keep the ally alive - Required for the best endings
  • • Trust is riskier - But leads to better outcomes if successful
  • • Reject legacy - The hardest choice but most rewarding
