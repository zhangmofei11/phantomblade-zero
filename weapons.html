Recommended Builds

Beginner Build - Longsword

  • • Weapon: Steel Longsword +3
  • • Playstyle: Balanced, focus on parrying
  • • Stats: Equal STR and SKI
  • • Skills: Deflect Master, Counter Strike

Speed Build - Dual Blades

  • • Weapon: Serpent Blades +5
  • • Playstyle: Aggressive, maintain pressure
  • • Stats: High SKI, some ARC for Sha-Chi
  • • Skills: Flowing Strikes, Spirit Drain

Punish Build - Greatsword

  • • Weapon: Mountain Crusher +4
  • • Playstyle: Wait for openings, heavy hits
  • • Stats: Max STR, some END
  • • Skills: Devastating Blow, Armor Break

Signature Build - Katana

  • • Weapon: Phantom Blade (unique) +5
  • • Playstyle: Precision, Iai counters
  • • Stats: High SKI, some STR
  • • Skills: Iai Strike, Moonlight Slash, Judgment
